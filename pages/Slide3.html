What is Nmap ?

”

Nmap is free and open-source, fast and easy to start with. You can scan one computer or a whole network, and you can save the results in a file for your reports.

”

Network administrators use it to check that nothing is open that should be closed. Ethical hackers use it as the first step of a security test, because you need to know what is running before you can look for weaknesses. That is why it is one of the most used tools in cybersecurity.
