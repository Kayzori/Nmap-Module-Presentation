What is Nmap ?

”

Nmap means “Network Mapper”. It is a free program that scans a network and shows what is on it. It works on Linux, Windows and macOS, and you use it by typing commands in the terminal.

”

Originally released in 1997 by Gordon Lyon (also known as Fyodor), it remains the gold standard tool for network discovery and security auditing.

”

It sends small messages to a computer and looks at the answers. From them, it can tell you if the computer is online, which ports are open and which programs are running.
